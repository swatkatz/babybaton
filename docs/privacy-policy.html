Baby Baton Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Baby Baton ("we", "our", or "the app") is a baby care tracking application designed to help caregivers log and share baby activities. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following data to provide the app's functionality:

  • Account information: Email address and password, used for authentication.
  • Baby care data: Feeding times, sleep logs, diaper changes, and other activities you choose to record.
  • Voice recordings: When you use voice input, audio is sent to OpenAI (Whisper) for transcription and to Anthropic (Claude) for parsing into structured data. We do not store audio recordings after processing.
  • Device information: Device identifiers used for local notifications and session management.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide baby care tracking and caregiver handoff features.
  • To process voice input into structured activity logs.
  • To send local device notifications (e.g., feeding reminders).

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the app's core functionality.

3. Data Sharing

We share data only as necessary to provide the service:

  • OpenAI: Voice audio is sent for transcription (Whisper API). Subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic: Transcribed text is sent for parsing (Claude API). Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Other caregivers in your family: Baby care data is shared with all caregivers who have joined your family within the app. This is a core feature of the app.

We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers hosted by Railway. We use HTTPS encryption for all data in transit. Voice recordings are processed in real time and are not retained after transcription.

5. Data Retention

Your baby care data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

Baby Baton is designed for use by adult caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The baby care data entered is provided by adult caregivers about infants in their care.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
